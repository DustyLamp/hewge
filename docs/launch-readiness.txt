Current campaign segments are size and country.

Required before accepting a binding bid
1. Complete seller/controller disclosures and the contract, privacy and permanent-ledger review in docs/legal-and-card-launch.txt with Swiss counsel, including applicable cross-border obligations. Do not present draft terms or mocked tests as legal approval.
2. Supply actual artist, provenance/rights, material/composition, size, measurements and final garment images. Set the real commission’s opening bid through docs/creative-commissioning.txt. After Hours remains the design study defined in docs/administrator-playbook.md.
3. Complete the administrator’s reveal record and individually chosen Swiss opening/closing dates. Align disclosures with the supported flow. No fabricated countdowns or silent rewriting of open-auction commitments.
4. Review insurable destinations, shipping charges, duties/taxes, delivery promises and target-market safety/label requirements. Country codes are an explicit allowlist, not an automated sanctions check. Shipping defaults to zero in a draft only; deliberately set it before scheduling.
5. Complete Swiss Stripe onboarding, card-only setup, Secrets Manager configuration, signed webhooks and every real sandbox case in docs/legal-and-card-launch.txt. Establish refund/dispute and stolen-object handling. Test the anticipated bidding load under docs/live-bidding.txt.
6. Obtain public email production access and commission delivery under docs/messaging-activation.txt, including the expected account-verification volume. Public release scheduling requires enabled transactional email. SMS is optional for the first launch and has its own approval/testing gates.
7. Publish approved terms via shared/legal.ts with a new immutable public/legal snapshot; retain acceptance-linked versions. Finish the review/test evidence before enabling the flags below.

Deployment gates
checkoutEnabled requires sellerTermsApproved=true, provenancePrivacyApproved=true and auctionCardTestsApproved=true, plus the Stripe secret and published terms URL. Activate only after the requirements above:
AWS_PROFILE=hewge npm run deploy:infra -- -c stripeSecretArn=<ARN> -c checkoutEnabled=true -c emailEnabled=true -c sellerTermsApproved=true -c provenancePrivacyApproved=true -c auctionCardTestsApproved=true -c termsUrl=https://hewge.design/terms
Retain these non-secret deployment choices locally on later deploys; omitted flags default to disabled. Never store credentials as CDK context. Flags attest to completed prerequisites; they are not review or test evidence themselves.

Additional activation paths
- NFC: after real hardware commissioning and physical tests, -c nfcCommissioned=true enables public verification. Follow docs/authentication.txt.
- Transfer-fee checkout: after the payment launch prerequisites and real fee/refund sandbox tests, -c transferPaymentsApproved=true together with checkoutEnabled=true enables it. Follow docs/permanent-provenance.txt; register the refund webhooks specified in docs/legal-and-card-launch.txt.
- Email/SMS: follow docs/messaging-activation.txt for the current account status, consent, budgets, provider approval, commissioning and separate flags. Provider approval alone never activates the application.

Use the explicit AWS deployment workflow in README.md. No gate is enabled merely by updating this checklist.
