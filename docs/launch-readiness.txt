HEWGE — AUCTION LAUNCH READINESS
13 October 2026

Implemented and safe to use now
- Public preview and dedicated journal page with shared typography. Piece/edition IDs remain; decorative section numbers are removed.
- Email-first membership with single-use confirmation. Sizes, location, bidding interest and optional profile/SMS preferences come after verification. SMS delivery/phone verification remain inactive.
- Collector sign-up with email verification, private preferences and owner dashboard.
- Owner account: jez@hewge.design, only after verified sign-up. Cognito admins group supports future authorised owners.
- Individual release dates. No standing weekly or Tuesday schedule.
- Highest valid bid wins. No paid entry, draw, random winner or follower weighting.
- Server-side bid validation and atomic head + bid + terms recording.
- Artist and material visibility controlled on the server, not merely hidden in HTML.
- Recipient-accepted, owner-reviewed transfers and a public provenance register. Physical NFC authentication is a documented next step, not implemented.
- Paid ownership defaults to anonymous. Buyer may choose a public name.
- Payment deadline, controlled next-bidder workflow, certificates and insured fulfilment checklist.

Required before accepting a binding bid
1. Legal seller name, address and support contact. jez@hewge.design was supplied for admin access only; do not assume it is the published legal support address.
2. Actual artist, provenance/rights, material/composition, size, measurements and final garment images.
3. Specific opening and closing dates. No fabricated countdowns. Once open, auction data cannot be rewritten.
4. Insurable destinations, shipping charge, duties/taxes, delivery promises and product safety/label requirements for target markets. Country codes are an explicit allowlist, not an automated sanctions check.
5. Swiss counsel review of the complete auction terms: contract formation, bid binding period, default notices/grace, cancellation and next-bidder offer, refunds/defects, applicable mandatory cross-border consumer law, privacy/controller disclosures. Mystery material/artist must be reviewed specifically: marketing suspense does not remove required product disclosures.
6. Swiss Stripe onboarding, TWINT/card activation and actual Stripe sandbox end-to-end tests, signed webhooks, asynchronous payment and expired-session recovery.
7. SES production access (requested and declined 13 October 2026; public verification delivery is blocked). Domain identity/DKIM are deployed. Verification email is queued only for the lifetime of its 48-hour link and reports pending while SES is in the sandbox. Release/winner emails remain disabled. The hewge-delivery configuration set enables bounce/complaint suppression and CloudWatch delivery metrics. The existing Cognito default email service can verify accounts with its own low daily sending quota; upgrade account email to SES after approval for larger launch volumes.
8. Publish final terms and replace the draft version in shared/auction.ts. Set deployment flags only after all checks above are complete.

Payments
A first place result is a payment offer, not a paid ownership claim. A signed Stripe paid event is required. Amount, currency, offer ID and winner identity must match. No shipment before payment. If a session creation fails ambiguously, the offer stays locked until Stripe reconciliation. Do not blindly remove checkoutStartedAt or assign a new winner. All amount fields are integer CHF centimes.

A 48-hour payment window and CHF 10 minimum bid increase are proposed editable defaults, not user-confirmed commercial terms. The first opening bid is CHF 339. Shipping fee defaults to zero in a draft only and must be deliberately set before scheduling.

Email and privacy operations
Review failed or ambiguous outbox sends in the studio before retrying. Avoid automatic resend after an ambiguous SES result. Release notifications exclude unverified/expired members and respect size preferences. The latest verified membership/account consent wins, including opt-outs. Transactional winner/receipt notices are independent of marketing consent. Unconfirmed membership requests/links expire in 48 hours, preference sessions in one hour and verified membership records in 180 days; auction/account retention must be finalised with the seller's policy. Owner can use the AWS console for account deletion requests while retaining required transaction evidence. Review SES bounce/complaint metrics and suppression lists; never turn on SMS sending until a provider, sender registration and phone-number verification are implemented.

Mystery assets
Identity fields are redacted from the public API until reveal. Files deliberately uploaded to public/images are PUBLIC: a teaser does not retract prior publication. Keep unreleased garment photography out of the public repo and bundle; add private storage and signed release-time delivery before using genuinely secret future artwork. Current provided After Hours artwork has already been approved for public preview.
