Implemented capabilities and activation limits
This inventory is not approval to enable every feature. Bidding, payment, public messaging and NFC have the separate gates below; gift registration, the full contributor model and book checkout remain to be designed/implemented.
- Public preview and dedicated journal page with shared typography. Use piece/edition IDs without decorative section numbering.
- Email-first membership with single-use confirmation. Sizes, location, bidding interest and optional profile/SMS preferences come after verification. SMS preferences and phone verification are implemented for restricted sandbox testing; public release texts remain inactive.
- Collector sign-up with email verification, private preferences and owner dashboard.
- Owner account: jez@hewge.design, only after verified sign-up. Cognito admins group supports future authorised owners.
- Individually chosen garment release dates. The annual book has a separate publication rhythm.
- Highest valid bid wins. No paid entry, draw, random winner or follower weighting.
- Server-side bid validation and atomic head + bid + terms recording.
- Artist and material visibility controlled on the server, not merely hidden in HTML. Administrators choose each release's reveal plan; the current binding-auction gate requires these details by opening and does not implement every intended sequence.
- Recipient-accepted, owner-reviewed transfers and a public provenance register. The NFC verifier is implemented but remains disabled pending real-tag commissioning.
- Paid ownership defaults to anonymous. Buyer may choose a public name.
- Card setup before bidding, immediate authorised winning charge, controlled failed-payment recovery, fresh optional next-bidder offers, certificates and insured fulfilment checklist.
- Versioned terms/privacy, protected acceptance evidence and a public withdrawal function with timestamped receipts and private review queue.
- See docs/legal-and-card-launch.txt for current legal, privacy, card and withdrawal activation requirements.

Payments
The auction winner owes payment at closing; ownership is registered only after confirmed payment. A signed Stripe paid event is required. Amount, currency, offer ID and winner identity must match. No shipment before payment. If a session creation fails ambiguously, the offer stays locked until Stripe reconciliation. Do not blindly remove checkoutStartedAt or assign a new winner. All amount fields are integer CHF centimes.

Payment is due immediately and the verified winner card is attempted at closing. The separate 24-hour technical recovery period is not credit or permission to defer payment. The default minimum bid increase is CHF 10. CHF 339 remains the After Hours design-study proposal; the first real commission's opening bid is undecided pending its cost review. Shipping fee defaults to zero in a draft only and must be deliberately set before scheduling.

Email and privacy operations
Review failed or ambiguous outbox sends in the studio before retrying. Avoid automatic resend after an ambiguous SES result. Release notifications exclude unverified/expired members and respect size preferences. The latest verified membership/account consent wins, including opt-outs. Transactional winner/receipt notices are independent of marketing consent. Unconfirmed membership requests/links expire in 48 hours, preference sessions in one hour and verified membership records in 180 days; auction/account retention must be finalised with the seller's policy. Owner can use the AWS console for account deletion requests while retaining required transaction evidence. Review SES bounce/complaint metrics and suppression lists; public SMS alerts remain disabled until real delivery testing, AWS production approval, destination requirements and the launch budget are reviewed. Restricted Swiss sandbox phone verification is deployed; see docs/messaging-activation.txt.

Influence, honestly applied
Reciprocity: offer meaningful access to the work and its actual creative process; agreed gifts and personal attention can contribute without creating an undisclosed obligation.
Scarcity: one real garment; no invented timers or stock.
Authority: credible creative expertise, recognised judgment, accurately attributed work and provenance at their agreed reveal stages; never invented credentials.
Consistency: voluntary size preferences and saved interests; no forced marketing consent.
Liking: the real voices and work of the collaborators and people who connect with it.
Social proof: the standing and perceived value Hewge develops in society, particularly its intended audience, through real recognition by influential and consequential people and the wider response. Genuine wear, recommendations, introductions, gifts, loans, cultural attention and purchases all contribute when accurately described. Follower totals or paid-owner counts alone do not define it; influence does not alter auction rules.
Unity: a shared connection to the work and its people, through following, creating, wearing, collecting or exploring the annual book.
Source: https://www.influenceatwork.com/7-principles-of-persuasion/
