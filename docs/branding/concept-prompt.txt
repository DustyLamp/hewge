Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: exploratory identity concept sheet for HEWGE, an independent Swiss art and fashion label selling unique artist-made garments. This is brainstorming, not a finished registered logo.
Primary request: design THREE genuinely different, memorable abstract symbol directions on one beautifully art-directed landscape contact sheet. The founder rejected a 1/1 logo as boring and too literal. No H monogram, no 1/1, no numbers in the marks, no shirt, hanger, crown, diamond, fingerprint, padlock, infinity symbol, starburst or stock luxury crest. Avoid the familiar generic AI four-point sparkle and tech startup loops. Do not explain scarcity visually. Be bold, strange, tactile and sophisticated, with the visual weight of a very heavy slab-serif wordmark.
Direction FOLD: a compact sculptural black silhouette, an asymmetric soft solid bending around a sharply cut negative space; ambiguous like a piece of folded material or a small modern sculpture. Only a few substantial contours, no shading.
Direction CUT: a forceful compact irregular block, confidently interrupted by one unexpected carved negative-space gesture; make the overall silhouette ownable and characterful, not a basic square split by a line or a letter.
Direction TRACE: a compact energetic single black brushlike gesture with surprising chunky proportions, a controlled handmade signature energy distilled into one reproducible silhouette; not literal handwriting or any letter.
Composition: cream/off-white #F0F0E9 background, three equal generously spaced columns. Each column has one large flat black mark at the top, a small same-mark cyan #00FEEF square avatar underneath, and a tiny 24px-like same-mark thumbnail to demonstrate legibility. Keep each direction internally consistent. Clear black silhouettes, near-square aspect, sufficiently simple for 24px favicon and monochrome embossing. No gradients, no photography, no mockup textures, no outlines, no shadows. Refined gallery presentation, not a busy moodboard.
Text: small HEWGE in the top left in a heavy eccentric slab-serif style; label the columns FOLD, CUT, TRACE below their main symbols, simple restrained sans serif. Only these four words. No taglines or explanatory prose.
Make this feel like a demanding independent identity designer's exploratory work, not generic corporate iconography.
