HEWGE — BRAND LETTERING AND ICONS

Identity
Use the Ultra HEWGE wordmark. Archivo Black is the heading face; Georgia italic provides the contrasting story/title treatment. Match the front page and story pages. Keep public copy sparse and imagery prominent.

The small icon is the H outlined from public/fonts/ultra-latin.woff2, with the homepage hero's 1.15 vertical scale. Brand teal is #00FEEF; ink is #111311. Sentence full stops use solid #00FEED without a muted tint.

Voice
Understated and specific. Let the work, withheld details and real dates suggest what comes next. Never narrate the marketing strategy, announce suspense or hype, promise a story is worth waiting for, or tell the reader how to feel. Keep auction obligations and reveal timing explicit. Use piece and edition numbers without decorative section numbering.

Book design
Apply this identity to the physical design and proofing process in docs/annual-book.md.

Icon assets
- public/favicon.svg: standalone path artwork with no external font dependency.
- public/favicon.png: 96px square for crawlers and browser fallback.
- public/favicon.ico: 16/32/48px embedded PNG images.
- public/apple-touch-icon.png: 180px square.
- public/hewge-icon.png: 512px square artwork.
- docs/branding/current-identity.svg: reference sheet for the wordmark and icon.

Regenerate PNG/ICO outputs with npm run assets:brand after editing the SVG. Serve icons at stable URLs and check image bodies, MIME types, square dimensions and crawler access after deployment. Search-engine display depends on crawling and indexing.

Letterform and licence
Source: Ultra Regular, copyright (c) 2010 Brian J. Bonislawsky DBA Astigmatic (AOETI), Apache 2.0. Keep public/fonts/Ultra-LICENSE.txt with the font and artwork. Archivo Black's SIL OFL is in public/fonts/ArchivoBlack-OFL.txt. Both fonts are self-hosted. These licences do not cover the Hewge artwork.

The H outline uses FontTools SVGPathPen with source bounds (41, 0, 1892, 1473). The 64px canvas centres a 44px-wide glyph, scales its height by 1.15 and leaves padding for circular search-result crops.
