HEWGE — LEGAL, PRIVACY AND CARD LAUNCH REVIEW
Policy alignment: 17 October 2026. Existing legal/technical review evidence below is from 14 October; this update does not establish new legal approval.

Status
The website has versioned terms/privacy text, a public withdrawal function, per-release card authorisation, card-only payment, immediate winning-charge code and a reviewed recovery process. This is an implementation for review, not a legal opinion or a claim that the terms are "bulletproof". Live bidding and payments remain disabled. No real Stripe account/sandbox or processor underwriting has been approved in this task. No country expansion has been activated.

Intended journeys and current scope
Use docs/administrator-playbook.md for current founder decisions. Administrators design the mystery separately for every release, potentially including a later artist reveal. The current binding-auction implementation requires artist/material details by opening. A different reveal plan needs the relevant disclosure review, new terms version where required and tested application support before use; the operational gate is not a universal creative policy.

The intended gift journey distinguishes payer, recipient and custodian. Current card consent uses the billing address for delivery and confirmed payment registers the paying winner. Direct gift delivery/recipient registration, surprise timing, declined gifts and initial-gift valuation require a specific supported process. Do not simulate payment or retrospectively rewrite accepted terms to make that process appear implemented. Subsequent transfers retain the recipient-paid 0.5% fee, verified acceptance, review and final approval; Hewge does not hold resale funds.

The annual purchasable book is a separate publication covering the year's pieces and their creative making, with deeper content than the website. Print rights, book purchase/fulfilment terms and the ordering flow remain to be completed; garment auction and transfer rules do not automatically apply. Optional future complimentary custodian copies, potentially signed by one or several artists, are not current entitlements. The book excludes internal operating/influence strategy and private customer/transaction journeys; clear the actual creative excerpts, images and credits for print rather than transferring whole private records.

Contract and returns
- Seller details are in shared/seller.ts. Confirm the exact legally required registered/trading name, address, contact methods and any VAT/company identifiers. EU-targeted sales may require a telephone/contact channel, EU product-safety economic operator, EU/UK privacy representative and additional local disclosures; do not invent these. Confirm whether each requirement applies before enabling that market.
- A primary winning sale forms at closing, payment due immediately. Each final bid records the account, amount, country, terms version/hash, mandate version/hash and card attempt in the same transaction as the auction head. Downloadable static snapshots are in public/legal and copied into transactional sale/winner notices. Build rejects a changed snapshot under an existing version. Future wording changes need a new version; preserve old files.
- Before opening binding bidding, complete sizing, measurements, actual material/artist details, rights/provenance, final photography, eligible countries, taxes/import treatment and insured delivery promises. Required information must be disclosed by opening under the reviewed plan; current software also requires artist/material details then. Earlier preview publication follows the administrator's reveal record and must not enable an unsupported commitment. Free-text tax disclosures are not a tax calculation engine or a VAT registration determination.
- No voluntary change-of-mind returns or size swaps. Preserve mandatory withdrawal, defect, misdescription, non-delivery and other non-waivable remedies. Online-only auction status and one-of-one status are not automatic exceptions from consumer withdrawal. The conditional EU/EEA/UK instructions and model form must be checked for the actual target markets and languages. Counsel should review Swiss CO auction/formation/default rules and enforceability of the bid wording.
- Under the 2026 EU online-withdrawal rules, /withdraw is linked in both footers and records a clear final confirmation without login or a reason. Name, confirmation email, contract reference and type are collected. The original timestamp and exact statement survive retries; a downloadable receipt and transactional email outbox are created. The notice is received when submitted, not when an operator later approves it. The form does not promise everyone a discretionary return right.
- The studio Withdrawals tab is a private case queue. Check it each working day and monitor the associated mail outbox. Verify the contract/claim without making necessary rights depend on excessive identity checks. Send return instructions promptly. Apply receipt-based statutory deadlines; do not substitute order date for delivery date. Refund within the applicable period, with only lawful withholding/diminished-value rules. Record customer communications, delivery/return evidence, decision and processor refund references. Do not close a case merely because an initial payment was valid. Card chargebacks must follow processor deadlines.
- A withdrawal case does not automatically revoke ownership, mark a unique garment available, cancel an in-flight payment or refund money. Reconcile first; freeze relevant verification/transfer as appropriate after validating the claim. Append any approved ownership correction. The auction-sale refund/dispute procedure is an operator process in Stripe; transfer-fee refunds have a separate existing API. No blanket no-refund/no-chargeback clause is used.
- A later bidder receives a NEW optional offer only after lawful termination of the first sale and payment reconciliation. Record evidence of any required notice/final opportunity and the termination basis. Never automatically charge a losing bidder. The later bidder expressly accepts before checkout; acceptance is audited.
- Transfer registration waits 14 days from acceptance unless the recipient explicitly requests early performance and acknowledges the loss of any service withdrawal right upon full performance. Both optional checkboxes are unselected. This does not waive rights for defective services. A paid transfer rejected on review requires a fee refund review, not automatic fee forfeiture. Resale funds are not held by Hewge.

Card/Stripe activation
- Card setup is per release/account. The user authorises storage/future winning payment, confirms adulthood and their authority to use the card, and explicitly chooses the billing address as delivery address. Hosted Stripe collects sensitive card details. The backend confirms the completed SetupIntent, customer, payment method, country, address completeness and card expiry. The browser redirect alone is not evidence.
- A card check does not reserve the auction amount, guarantee sufficient funds, identify the legal owner or eliminate future authentication. Do not advertise it as a guaranteed ability to pay. Stripe SetupIntent usage must be off_session. Confirm card-only configuration, Stripe terms URL and SCA behaviour in the actual account/API version.
- The worker settles normally within one minute and attempts the saved winner card for the server-calculated bid plus shipping. The persistent attempt is created BEFORE the Stripe call. Concurrent/retried calls use the same idempotency key and parameters. After 23 hours an ambiguous missing intent ID needs operator reconciliation; never create a replacement charge blindly.
- A successful signed payment_intent.succeeded webhook must match amount, currency, customer, auction, winner, offer and attempt. Automatic recovery also retrieves the Stripe object server-side. The atomic paid transition registers anonymous ownership. An unsettled intent blocks fallback payment. A definitively failed/authentication-required intent is cancelled before hosted checkout permits a new payment. Hosted fallback is card-only and restricted to the same eligible country. Only a signed confirmed payment registers ownership.
- The 24-hour recovery timestamp is not a contractual instalment or a right to delay. It prevents immediate reassignment during SCA, provider/network failures and manual reconciliation. Notice and lawful termination requirements still apply after that period; the timestamp alone does not terminate a sale.
- Configure webhook events: checkout.session.completed, checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded, payment_intent.succeeded, refund.created and refund.updated (last two support transfer fees). Use the deployed WebhookUrl and Secrets Manager; never commit credentials.
- Required real sandbox cases: card setup succeeds/fails/requires SCA; wrong customer/address/country; stale setup terms; simultaneous bid/card setup; winner charge success, decline and 3DS; crash after Stripe success before DDB receipt; duplicate and out-of-order webhook; network ambiguity across retries; intent processing/cancellation race; expired checkout; subsequent optional offer acceptance; full/partial refund and dispute; one owner, one paid amount and correct receipt in all outcomes. Unit tests do not replace these cases.

Privacy and permanent history
- Necessary private purchase-to-account and current-owner links can support contract performance, fraud prevention and ownership services without making marketing compulsory. Document the actual GDPR Article 6 basis by purpose, Swiss FADP requirements, the legitimate-interest balancing assessment, recipients and international transfers. Do not use a catch-all consent checkbox to legitimise all processing.
- Pseudonymous references, email hashes and transaction fingerprints can still be personal data. "Forever" is not an exception from minimisation, storage limitation or data-subject rights. The existing audit archive has 100-year compliance locks plus indefinite holds; already-locked objects cannot be shortened. Review every permanent event field, including security events and operation digests, before enabling real sales. Complete a privacy-risk/DPIA assessment as appropriate and obtain specialist advice on necessity. If the design cannot be justified, minimise/redesign FUTURE records before activation; never pretend existing locked data is routinely erasable. The user wants durable object provenance, not an undisclosed waiver of privacy rights.
- Keep names/emails/addresses, Stripe references and uploaded evidence separate from protected event payloads. Maintain the minimum identity link needed for current registered ownership and legal/accounting claims. Optional profile/public-name/marketing choices remain removable. Review inactive accounts, unsuccessful bids, expired card setups, previous-owner links and evidence at least quarterly; document necessity, review dates and deletion/restriction outcomes. Accounting records normally require ten years from financial-year end under Swiss rules; that does not make every photo, profile or IP record an accounting voucher. Dispute holds must be specific and reviewed.
- /me/delete deletes an eligible optional profile and suppresses older marketing; it does not silently delete Cognito login, Stripe data, ownership or legally necessary records. Full access/portability/erasure/restriction requests go to the published privacy contact for verified handling. Respond within applicable deadlines (normally one month GDPR / 30 days Swiss, with justified extensions). Supply an understandable copy of requester data without disclosing another person's private data. Explain retained categories and the legal reason; do not refuse every request merely because some records must remain.
- Before launch document processors/subprocessors and AWS/Stripe roles, DPAs, actual processing countries, adequacy/certification applicability, SCCs/Swiss addenda and supplementary measures. Stockholm storage alone does not prevent international access or CDN processing. No EU/UK representative, certification or adequacy coverage is claimed as already arranged.
- Ordinary backups cycle over 35 days. Maintain a restricted deletion/suppression register so restores do not revive deleted optional data or subscriptions. Locked audit copies have the separate irreversibility described above. Test privacy exports, profile/public-name removal, marketing opt-out after contact expiry and restored suppression.
- Set up a breach response owner/process: contain, preserve relevant evidence, assess risk and notify the FDPIC where required; GDPR authority notification normally within 72 hours where the risk threshold is met, and affected people without undue delay where required. No blanket assurance of breach-proof or bank-certified security is made.

Deployment gates
checkoutEnabled requires sellerTermsApproved=true, provenancePrivacyApproved=true and auctionCardTestsApproved=true, plus the Stripe secret and published terms URL. These flags are attestations to completed work, not substitutes for it. Public release scheduling also requires enabled transactional email. Per-release destination allowlists, mandatory details, tax and delivery disclosures remain required. Do not enable any gate using mocked tests as evidence. No flags are enabled by this change.
