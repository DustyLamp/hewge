# Asset provenance

The seven files in `originals/` are the approved supplied painted mockup and print-panel proofs for the After Hours design study. Keep these source images intact. WebP files in `public/images/` are resized and encoded presentation copies, reproducible with `npm run assets`.

`shirt-front-cutout.png` is an AI-derived presentation cutout of `originals/00-approved-painted-mockup.png`. Its optimised web asset is `public/images/shirt-front.webp`. The approved mockup is the gallery design reference. The cutout can vary in small printed details and must not be used as manufacturing artwork.

These assets present a design study. Final garment photography, material, measurements and creator information are required for a real release.
